Biography

A Norwegian eye for the American landscape.

Gregory Olav grew up between the fjords and forests of southern Norway, where architecture is less a statement than a negotiation — with weather, with light, with a landscape that does not forgive careless building. That upbringing became a method: study the place first, then draw.

After completing his Master of Architecture at the Oslo School of Architecture and Design, Gregory spent his early career on civic and residential work across Scandinavia, developing the disciplined, material-honest approach the region is known for. A commission in the United States changed the trajectory of his practice: he found American clients hungry for exactly that discipline, and American sites that rewarded it.

In 2011 he founded Gregory Olav Architectural & Consulting LLC — a firm deliberately structured to work on both shores. The design DNA remains Norwegian; the delivery expertise is thoroughly American, fluent in U.S. codes, permitting, and construction culture.

Today Gregory divides his time between the drafting table and the field. He still reviews every drawing set the firm issues, and he still answers his own phone.

Philosophy

Listen first. Draw second. Build once.

Gregory's consulting practice is built on a simple conviction: most expensive mistakes in a building are made in conversations that never happened. So he starts every engagement by listening — to the client, the site, the budget, and the builder.

His design philosophy follows three commitments: buildings should age with dignity, materials should be what they appear to be, and daylight is a structural element, not a finish.

The Path

From Oslo to New York.

  1. 2003

    Oslo School of Architecture and Design

    Graduates with a Master of Architecture, with a thesis on long-life timber construction in harsh climates.

  2. 2003–2010

    Practice across Scandinavia

    Civic, cultural, and residential work in Norway and Denmark; project architect on award-shortlisted public buildings.

  3. 2011

    Founds the firm in Oslo

    Gregory Olav Architectural & Consulting is established with a mandate to carry Nordic design standards abroad.

  4. 2014

    First U.S. commission

    A private residence in the Hudson Valley opens the American chapter of the practice.

  5. 2017

    U.S. headquarters, New York

    The LLC establishes its American base and begins serving developer and institutional clients nationwide.

  6. Today

    One practice, two shores

    Over 120 projects delivered, with active work in New York, the Midwest, and the South — and design direction still rooted in Oslo.

Family

The people behind the practice.

The firm carries one name, but it is held up by a family. Gregory is married to Ingrid Olav, and together they have three children — Sofie, Magnus, and Astrid. Home life spans the same two shores as the practice — school years in New York, summers on the Norwegian coast.

It is a family that keeps the work honest: every house the studio draws is measured against the question of whether Gregory would raise his own children in it.
