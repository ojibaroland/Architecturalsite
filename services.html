How We Work

A process with no surprises.

  1. i.

    Listen

    Every engagement begins with a conversation led by Gregory — about the site, the brief, the budget, and what the building needs to do for the next fifty years.

  2. ii.

    Study

    Feasibility, zoning, climate, and daylight analysis before any design is drawn. We tell you early if something can't or shouldn't be built.

  3. iii.

    Design

    Concept through construction documents, reviewed personally by the principal at every milestone. Nothing leaves the studio unexamined.

  4. iv.

    Deliver

    Permitting, bidding, and construction administration — with the firm on site defending the design until the keys are handed over.

  5. v.

    Revisit

    A post-occupancy review one year in. Buildings teach; we make sure the lessons come home.

The Nordic Advantage

Why a Norwegian firm, for an American project?

Norway builds for a hundred-year horizon in one of the world's harshest climates, under some of its strictest energy codes. That produces habits — envelope-first design, honest materials, ruthless daylighting discipline — that translate directly into lower operating costs and longer-lived buildings anywhere in the United States.
