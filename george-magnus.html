About

The drafting table and the job site, one desk apart.

Most building problems are born in the gap between the people who design and the people who build. George B Magnus has spent his career closing that gap — practicing as both architect and construction engineer, so the drawing and the structure are never strangers to each other.

Working from Norway, George's practice is shaped by rigorous building standards and demanding climate conditions: wind that tests every connection, temperature extremes that test every envelope, and structural loads that test every foundation. It is an environment that rewards exactly the kind of building George B Magnus Structural Group exists to do — designed with intent, engineered without shortcuts, and built to be handed to the next generation.

Clients work with George directly. He leads the first conversation, draws and reviews the documents, and walks the site through construction — one accountable professional from concept to completion.

Philosophy

Design what can be built. Build what was designed.

George's conviction is simple: the most expensive mistakes in a building are made when the architect and the engineer stop talking. In this practice they never start being separate people — every line on the drawing already knows what it weighs, what it costs, and how it goes up.

Three commitments run through the work: structures should be honest about how they stand, buildings should age with dignity, and the owner should never be the last to know.

Areas of Practice

What George brings to a project.

  1. 01

    Architectural design

    Concept through construction documents for homes, workplaces, and civic buildings — designed around how people will actually use them.

  2. 02

    Structural engineering

    Structural systems selected and sized for the real loads a building will see — wind, weather, and time included.

  5. 05

    Owner advisory

    Senior counsel through permitting, bidding, and construction — an experienced advocate on the owner's side of the table.
