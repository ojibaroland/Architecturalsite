Selected Work

Projects across the United States.

A representative selection of design and consulting work. Full project documentation and references are shared during consultation.

Fjordline Residence

Private Home · Hudson Valley, NY

A timber-and-glass family home that borrows the section of a Norwegian boathouse and opens it to the river. Cross-laminated timber structure, passive-house envelope, and a roofline tuned to winter sun angles.

Meridian Commons

Workplace · Chicago, IL

A mid-rise workplace whose facade rhythm and daylighting follow Scandinavian office standards — no desk more than seven meters from a window, and an envelope that beat local energy code by 40%.

Solhaven Courtyard Homes

Residential Development · Austin, TX

Twelve courtyard houses arranged around shared gardens — a Nordic co-housing model adapted to the Texas sun, with deep eaves doing the work that triple glazing does in Oslo.

Nordlys Cultural Pavilion

Civic · Minneapolis, MN

A glulam-arch pavilion for a Nordic-American cultural society — a public room shaped like the northern lights it is named for, engineered for Minnesota snow loads.

Harbor Mill

Adaptive Reuse · Portland, ME

A 19th-century waterfront mill converted to studios and light industry. Consulting engagement: feasibility, envelope strategy, and design review for the architect of record.

Askeladden Campus

Master Plan · Research Triangle, NC

A phased master plan for a technology campus, organized around a shared commons rather than parking — walkable in the Nordic manner, feasible in the American one.
