The Magnus Way

The architect and the engineer are the same person.

Most projects split design and engineering across separate firms — and pay for it in change orders, compromises, and blame. At George B Magnus Structural Group, the hand that draws the building is the hand that engineers it. Every design decision is made with the structure, the budget, and the build sequence already in mind — shaped by hard-weather country where wind, heat, and shifting soils punish careless building.
